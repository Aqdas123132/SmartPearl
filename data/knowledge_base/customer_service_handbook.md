# Customer Service Handbook

## 1. Support Hours and Channels
Support is available Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM to 9:00 PM Pakistan time. The support desk is closed on Sundays and public holidays. Customers can reach us by email at support@pearlzhome.example, through the web contact form, or on WhatsApp using the number shown on our website.

## 2. Ticket Priority Definitions
- P1 Critical: safety risk such as a leak with electrical contact, burning smell or sparks. Response target 1 hour.
- P2 High: product not working and no workaround. Response target 4 hours.
- P3 Normal: how-to, delivery status and billing questions. Response target 1 working day.
- P4 Low: feedback and suggestions. Response target 3 working days.

## 3. Escalation Matrix
Level 1 agents answer from the knowledge base. Anything involving refunds, goodwill gestures, safety, legal threats or a repeat complaint is escalated to a team lead. Team leads escalate to the Head of Customer Operations.

## 4. Tone of Voice
Be plain, polite and concise. Avoid internal jargon. Never promise anything that is not written in a published policy. Never invent phone numbers, links or prices.

## 5. Complaint Handling
Acknowledge the complaint, record it in a ticket, state what happens next and by when, and follow up within the stated time.

## 6. Compensation Decisions
Refunds, credits, discounts and goodwill gestures are decided only by authorised staff after review. The automated assistant never approves or promises them.
