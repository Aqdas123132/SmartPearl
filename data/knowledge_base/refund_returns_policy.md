# Refund and Returns Policy

## 1. Return Window
You may return a product for any reason within 14 days of the delivery date. After 14 days, returns are not accepted, but warranty rights still apply (see the Warranty Policy).

## 2. Condition Requirements
### 2.1 Unopened items
Items in sealed, original packaging are eligible for a full refund of the product price.

### 2.2 Opened but unused items
An appliance that has been opened but not installed or used, with all accessories and original packaging, is eligible for a refund minus a restocking fee of 10% of the product price.

### 2.3 Installed or used items
An appliance that has been installed, connected to water or used is not eligible for a change-of-mind return. If it is faulty, the Warranty Policy applies instead.

### 2.4 Filter cartridges
Filter cartridges can be returned only if the sealed packaging is unopened. Opened filter cartridges are non-refundable for hygiene reasons.

## 3. Who Pays Return Shipping
- Change-of-mind returns: the customer pays the return courier cost.
- Wrong item, damaged in transit or dead on arrival: Pearlz pays the return shipping. Report these within 7 days of delivery with photos.

## 4. Refund Processing Time
Refunds are issued to the original payment method after the returned item passes inspection at our warehouse.
- Credit or debit card: 7 to 10 working days.
- Bank transfer: 5 working days.
- JazzCash or Easypaisa wallet: 3 working days.
- Cash on delivery orders: refunded by bank transfer within 5 working days.

## 5. Non-Refundable Items
Opened filter cartridges, installation fees once the technician has visited, descaling kits that have been opened, and gift cards are non-refundable.

## 6. Partial Refunds and Restocking Fees
A restocking fee of 10% applies to opened but unused appliances. If an item is returned with missing accessories, the cost of the missing parts is deducted from the refund.

## 7. Return, Replacement and Warranty Repair
- A return is the customer's choice to give the product back for a refund within 14 days.
- A replacement is issued when a product arrives faulty or damaged within 7 days of delivery.
- A warranty repair fixes a manufacturing defect after the 14-day window, under the Warranty Policy.

## 8. Promotional Bundles
Bundles must be returned as a complete set. If a free item from a bundle is not returned, its list price is deducted from the refund.

## 9. Refund Decisions
Refunds are decided by Pearlz staff after inspection. Neither this document nor the support assistant can promise a refund amount before inspection.
