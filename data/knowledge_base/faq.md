# Frequently Asked Questions

## Ordering and Payment
### Q1. Can I order by phone?
Orders are placed through our online store or an authorised retail partner. Our support team can help you through the checkout by email or WhatsApp.

### Q2. What payment methods do you accept?
We accept credit and debit cards, bank transfer, JazzCash, Easypaisa and cash on delivery.

### Q3. Can I cancel my order after placing it?
You can cancel before the order is dispatched by emailing support@pearlzhome.example. After dispatch, use the 14-day return rules in the Refund and Returns Policy.

### Q4. Do I get an invoice?
Yes. An invoice is emailed when the order is dispatched. Please keep it, because your warranty period is counted from the invoice date.

## Delivery
### Q5. How long will my parcel take to arrive?
Metro cities take 1 to 2 working days, secondary cities 3 to 4 working days and remote areas 5 to 7 working days.

### Q6. Can I get my order tomorrow?
Next-day delivery is available only in Karachi, Lahore and Islamabad, for orders placed before 2:00 PM.

### Q7. Is delivery free?
Delivery is free on orders of PKR 25,000 or more. Below that, a flat fee of PKR 350, PKR 600 or PKR 1,200 applies depending on where you live.

### Q8. I was not home when the courier came. What now?
The courier tries twice, then holds the parcel for 5 days. Contact support to arrange another delivery.

### Q9. Do you deliver abroad?
Not at the moment. We deliver within Pakistan only.

## Installation
### Q10. Can I install the purifier myself?
Yes, if you follow the Installation Guide. Cutting or changing the internal tubing, or opening the housing, can void your warranty.

### Q11. How much is professional installation?
PKR 2,500 in metro cities, PKR 3,500 in secondary cities and PKR 5,000 in remote areas. Book through support.

### Q12. What water pressure does the purifier need?
Between 15 and 80 psi.

### Q13. Do I need to run the water before drinking it the first time?
Yes. New units must be flushed before first use. See the first-run flushing section of the Installation Guide.

## Filters
### Q14. How often should I change the filters?
For the AquaPearl 500 Pro: sediment every 4 months, carbon every 8 months and membrane every 18 months.

### Q15. My water tastes strange after I changed a filter. Is something wrong?
This is usually air or carbon dust from the new filter. Flush the system as described in the Installation Guide.

### Q16. Where do I buy replacement filters?
From our online store or authorised retail partners. See the Pricing Guide for the part numbers.

### Q17. Can I use another brand's filters?
We only support Pearlz filters. Using other filters is not covered by our documentation, and damage they cause is not covered by the warranty.

### Q18. What does the red flashing light mean?
It means a filter needs replacing. Count the beeps to find the code, then see the Troubleshooting Guide.

## Warranty and Returns
### Q19. How long is my warranty?
The AquaPearl 500 Pro has 24 months. The AquaPearl 300 and AeroPearl Mini have 12 months. It is counted from the invoice date.

### Q20. Does the warranty cover filters?
No. Filters are consumables and are not covered.

### Q21. Can I return a purifier I changed my mind about?
Yes, within 14 days of delivery. Unopened items get a full refund. Opened but unused items have a 10% restocking fee.

### Q22. How long does a refund take?
Between 3 and 10 working days depending on the payment method, after the item passes inspection.

### Q23. Who pays for return shipping?
You do for change-of-mind returns. Pearlz pays if the item was wrong, damaged or dead on arrival.

### Q24. I sold my purifier. Can the new owner keep the warranty?
Yes, once. The new owner must contact support within 30 days with a copy of the invoice.

### Q25. Will you pay for damage to my cabinet if my unit leaks?
The Warranty Policy excludes damage to property. Please contact support and your home insurer.

## App and Account
### Q26. How do I connect the app?
Turn on Bluetooth, use a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network and hold the pairing button for 3 seconds.

### Q27. Which data does the app collect?
Account email, device information and water-usage telemetry. See the Privacy Policy for details.

### Q28. How do I delete my account and data?
Email privacy@pearlzhome.example from your registered address. We reply within 30 days.

### Q29. How do I check my firmware version?
Open the app, go to Device and then About.

### Q30. What are your support hours?
Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM to 9:00 PM Pakistan time.

### Q31. How do I contact a human?
Email support@pearlzhome.example, use the web contact form or message us on WhatsApp.
