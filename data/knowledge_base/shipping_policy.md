# Shipping Policy

## 1. Delivery Timelines
Delivery times are counted in working days (Monday to Saturday, excluding public holidays) from the dispatch date. Pearlz groups destinations into three tiers.

| Tier | Examples | Delivery window | Flat shipping fee |
|---|---|---|---|
| Metro | Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad, Rawalpindi | 1 to 2 working days | PKR 350 |
| Secondary city | Faisalabad, Multan, Peshawar, Quetta, Hyderabad, Sialkot | 3 to 4 working days | PKR 600 |
| Remote area | Skardu, Gilgit, Hunza, Chitral, Gwadar, Kalam | 5 to 7 working days | PKR 1,200 |

## 2. Dispatch Cut-off
Orders placed and paid before 2:00 PM Pakistan time, Monday to Saturday, are dispatched the same day. Later orders are dispatched the next working day.

## 3. Free Shipping
Orders of PKR 25,000 or more qualify for free standard shipping to all tiers.

## 4. Express and Next-Day Delivery
Next-day delivery is available only in Karachi, Lahore and Islamabad, for orders placed before the 2:00 PM cut-off. Next-day delivery is not offered to secondary cities or remote areas.

## 5. Packaging and Fragile Items
Appliances are double-boxed with foam corners. Please inspect the parcel at delivery and report visible damage within 48 hours with photos.

## 6. Failed Delivery Attempts
The courier makes two delivery attempts. After the second failed attempt the parcel is held at the courier hub for 5 days and is then returned to Pearlz. Re-delivery after a return to the warehouse is charged at the standard shipping fee again.

## 7. International Shipping
Pearlz does not currently ship outside Pakistan.

## 8. Tracking
A tracking link is sent by SMS and email within 24 hours of dispatch.
