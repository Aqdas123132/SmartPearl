# Troubleshooting Guide

This guide covers the AquaPearl 500 Pro, AquaPearl 300 and AeroPearl Mini. Alert codes below refer to firmware 2.4 and later. For the full list of codes see the product manual.

## 1. Symptom, Cause and Action Table
| # | Symptom | Probable cause | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | No power, no lights | Socket or cable fault | Check the socket with another device, reseat the power cable, then try again. If still dead, contact support. |
| 2 | Low water flow | Blocked sediment filter or low inlet pressure | Check that the inlet valve is fully open. If the sediment filter is more than 4 months old, replace it. |
| 3 | Water under the base or leak sensor alarm | Loose tube clip, damaged O-ring or internal leak | Close the inlet, unplug the unit and do not use it. See the escalation section below. |
| 4 | Bad taste or odour | Carbon filter expired or unit unused for a long time | Replace the carbon filter if it is older than 8 months. Sanitise the tank as described in the manual. |
| 5 | Pump runs continuously | Tank-full sensor dirty or inlet leaking | Switch off, wipe the sensor area, restart. If it continues, contact support. |
| 6 | Three short beeps and a flashing red light (error E-03) | Sediment pre-filter is saturated | Replace the sediment filter (FLT-S5), then press and hold RESET for 5 seconds. |
| 7 | Four beeps and flashing red light (E-04) | Carbon filter has reached its limit | Replace the carbon filter (FLT-C5), then hold RESET for 5 seconds. |
| 8 | Five beeps and steady red light (E-05) | RO membrane is saturated | Replace the membrane (FLT-M5) and hold RESET for 5 seconds. |
| 9 | Blinking red LED with no beeps | Filter life counter reached 100% | Replace the filter indicated on the display and reset. |
| 10 | TDS reading is high | Membrane is exhausted or a new filter has not been flushed | Flush the system. If the reading stays high, replace the membrane. |
| 11 | Noisy pump | Air in the line or low inlet pressure | Open the tap for a minute to purge air. Check inlet pressure is within 15 to 80 psi. |
| 12 | App will not pair | Phone Bluetooth off or router on 5 GHz only | Enable Bluetooth, use a 2.4 GHz network and hold the pairing button for 3 seconds. |
| 13 | Unit shuts off by itself | Pump overheating (E-07) | Switch off for 30 minutes. If it repeats, contact support. |
| 14 | Water is warm | Unit placed next to a heat source | Move the unit at least 30 cm away from stoves or heaters. |
| 15 | Tank will not fill | Inlet closed or low pressure (E-01) | Open the inlet valve. If pressure is below 15 psi, a booster pump may be needed. |
| 16 | White particles in water | Fresh carbon fines or scale | Flush the system. Descale if the unit has not been descaled for 3 months. |

## 2. Escalation: Do Not Attempt These Yourself
Stop using the product and contact support@pearlzhome.example for any of the following:
- Water leaking inside or from under the base (E-06).
- Burning smell, smoke, sparks or a tripped circuit breaker.
- Error E-08 (control board communication fault).
- Any problem that would require opening the housing.
Opening the housing yourself or having a non-Pearlz technician do it is an unauthorised repair under the Warranty Policy.
