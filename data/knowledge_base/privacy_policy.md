# Privacy Policy

## 1. Who We Are
Pearlz Home Systems (Pvt.) Ltd. is the controller of the personal data described here. Privacy requests go to privacy@pearlzhome.example.

## 2. Data We Collect
### 2.1 At purchase
Name, delivery address, phone number, email address, order details and the payment method used. Card numbers are handled by our payment processor and are not stored by Pearlz.

### 2.2 Through the mobile app
Account email, device model, app version, Bluetooth pairing information and the serial number of paired units.

### 2.3 Water-usage telemetry
Connected units send daily water volume, filter life counters, error codes, firmware version and TDS readings. Telemetry is linked to your account so that filter reminders work. It does not include location tracking.

## 3. Retention Periods
- Account data: kept while the account is active and deleted within 90 days of account closure.
- Order and invoice records: 6 years, for tax reasons.
- Water-usage telemetry: 24 months.
- Support tickets and chat transcripts: 3 years.

## 4. Who We Share Data With
- Courier partners: name, address and phone number for delivery.
- Payment processors: payment details to take payment and issue refunds.
- Cloud hosting providers: store app and telemetry data on our behalf.
Pearlz does not sell personal data.

## 5. Your Rights
You may ask to access, correct, delete or export your personal data. Email privacy@pearlzhome.example from your registered address. We respond within 30 days. Some records, such as invoices, must be kept for the legal retention period.

## 6. Cookies
Our website uses essential cookies for the cart and login, and optional analytics cookies that you can decline in the cookie banner.
