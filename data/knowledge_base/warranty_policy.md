# Warranty Policy

## 1. Overview
This Warranty Policy applies to products sold by Pearlz Home Systems (Pvt.) Ltd. ("Pearlz") through its online store and authorised retail partners. It explains what Pearlz repairs or replaces free of charge when a product fails because of a manufacturing defect.

## 2. Warranty Periods
| Product | Warranty period |
|---|---|
| AquaPearl 500 Pro | 24 months |
| AquaPearl 300 | 12 months |
| AeroPearl Mini | 12 months |
| AquaPearl 200 (discontinued) | 12 months, with extended cover (see section 7) |
| AeroPearl One (discontinued) | 12 months, with extended cover (see section 7) |

The warranty period is counted from the date printed on the purchase invoice, not from the delivery date. Keep your invoice: it is the only accepted proof of the start date.

## 3. What Is Covered
### 3.1 Covered components
Pearlz repairs or replaces, at its discretion, the following parts if they fail because of a manufacturing defect: the purifier body and housing, the water pump, the motor, the electronic control board and the UV-LED module.

### 3.2 Exclusions
The warranty does not cover:
- Consumables: filter cartridges, sediment filters, carbon filters, RO membranes, tubing, O-rings and clips. These wear out through normal use and are replaced at the customer's cost.
- Physical damage, such as drops, impact, cracks caused by force, or frozen water inside the unit.
- Unauthorised repair: any repair, opening of the housing or modification carried out by anyone who is not a Pearlz-authorised technician. This exclusion applies even if the warranty period has not ended, and it applies to the affected component.
- Commercial use: use in restaurants, offices, clinics, shops or any setting other than a private household.
- Water-quality or supply damage: failures caused by inlet water above 1,500 ppm TDS, or by water pressure outside the 15 to 80 psi range stated in the product manual.
- Consequential damage: Pearlz does not compensate for damage to property (for example kitchen cabinets, floors or appliances) or for loss of use caused by a product failure. Customers who suffer a leak may wish to contact their home insurer.

## 4. How the Warranty Period Is Calculated
The period starts on the invoice date. Repairs and replacements under warranty do not extend or restart the period. A replacement unit carries the remaining period of the original unit.

## 5. Transfer on Resale
The warranty may be transferred once to a second owner. The second owner must contact support within 30 days of taking ownership and provide a copy of the original invoice. The transfer does not restart the period.

## 6. How to Make a Claim
Warranty claims are handled under the claim procedure in the Pearlz Service and Maintenance Agreement. Please contact support@pearlzhome.example and have your invoice, the unit serial number and photos or a short video of the problem ready.

## 7. Extended Warranty for Discontinued Models
Owners of the discontinued AquaPearl 200 and AeroPearl One receive extended cover of 36 months from the invoice date for the pump and motor only. All other components keep the standard 12-month period. Consumables are never covered.

## 8. Changes to This Policy
Pearlz may revise this policy quarterly. The version in force on the invoice date applies to your purchase.
